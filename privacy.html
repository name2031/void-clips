Honest summary

VOID Clips is a demo that runs locally in your browser. There is no real account server. Almost everything stays on your device via localStorage.

What we store (on your device)

  • Display name / email you type at sign-in
  • Free generation credits
  • Style / language / aspect prefs
  • Recent pasted links
  • Waitlist emails you submit (local list only)
  • Bug reports you submit in-app (local list only)
  • UI flags (onboarding dismissed, beta banner dismissed)

What we do not do

  • No cloud sync of your account
  • No analytics backend in this demo
  • No real payment or card data
  • No selling of personal data (there is no server-side profile)

Third parties

This static demo may load Google Fonts. TikTok links open TikTok’s site under their policies. Clearing site data / localStorage removes demo data from this browser.
