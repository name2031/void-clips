Privacy

Last updated: Sep 2026 · Public launch

Honest summary

VOID Clips stores account prefs and demo state in your browser. The hosted API may process email (verification via Resend), Swish payment refs, and Stripe checkout emails / session IDs to grant Pro. We do not sell personal data.

What we store (on your device)

  • Email / display name you type at sign-in
  • Salted password hash (never plaintext)
  • Free generation credits and Pro entitlement flags
  • Style / language / aspect prefs
  • Recent pasted links
  • Waitlist emails and local help notes you submit
  • UI flags (onboarding / banner dismissed)

What the server may process

  • Email + one-time codes for verify / password reset (Resend)
  • Stripe Checkout session metadata (email, plan) when you pay by card
  • Manual Swish pending refs (email, amount, plan) until owner confirms or rejects
  • Pro grant records after confirmed payment

Payments

Card data is handled by Stripe — we never see full card numbers. Swish payments go to the owner’s Swish number; we only store the reference you use so Pro can be confirmed.

Third parties

Google Fonts may load in the browser. TikTok links open TikTok under their policies. Stripe and Resend process data under their terms when those features are used.
